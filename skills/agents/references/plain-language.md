# Plain Language

Applies when: the user says "说人话", or an explanation has drifted into jargon, buzzwords, or academic phrasing that a normal person would not use.

Execute the following instruction (verbatim):

> 说人话

Literally "speak human": say it the way you would explain it to a colleague face to face.

Key points:

- Drop the jargon, buzzwords, and textbook definitions; use everyday words and concrete examples.
- Short sentences, direct statements. Say what it is, why it matters, and what to do — nothing else.
- If a technical term is unavoidable, immediately explain it in one plain sentence.
- Plain does not mean vague: keep the content accurate, just strip away the packaging.
